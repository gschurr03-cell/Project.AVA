# AVA Recommendation safety policy

## Safety tiers

- Tier 1: observation, recording, evidence collection, monitoring, and preservation
- Tier 2: cautious technical cue or low-risk drill with compatible context
- Tier 3: training consideration or qualified coach discussion
- Tier 4: professional medical review

The engine selects the lowest sufficient tier. Tier 4 requires explicit athlete-reported
pain, injury, or limitation and cannot result from biomechanics alone.

## Volume policy

MVP guidance uses structured categories only:

- observation only, low intensity, submaximal, or coach determined;
- not applicable, a few quality repetitions, or coach determined;
- full recovery where activity is used;
- single review, limited exposure, or coach determined.

No exact program, weekly schedule, high-speed load, or maximal dosage is generated.

## Stop conditions

Every output includes stopping for pain or increasing discomfort, substantial technique
or coordination deterioration, dizziness, unsafe surface, or unstable equipment. Drill
entries may add specific conditions.

AVA does not supervise the session in real time.

## Contraindications

Universal contraindications include active pain, acute injury, clinician restrictions,
unsafe surface, unsuitable footwear, insufficient warm-up, and excessive fatigue.
Context is never inferred from biomechanics.

## Language and claim safety

Generated recommendation language rejects claims including guaranteed improvement,
injury risk, “fix your imbalance,” cure/prevention language, and exact time or speed
improvements.

Every recommendation stores exclusions stating that it does not guarantee faster sprint
times, diagnose weakness or mobility restrictions, replace a coach or medical assessment,
establish injury risk, or prescribe a full program.
