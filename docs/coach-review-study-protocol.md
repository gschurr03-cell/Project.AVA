# Coach review study protocol

Use qualified, independently rating sprint coaches and blind them to the desired outcome.
Record coaching years, athlete level/events, strength/biomechanics background,
certifications, conflicts and AVA relationship. Review source evidence first, then Root
Cause, recommendations, priorities and report language.

Measure coach-to-coach and AVA-to-coach top-one/top-three agreement, accept/edit/reject,
usefulness, safety, clarity, trust, actionability and evidence requests. Categorize
disagreement as terminology, priority, missing context, metric/interpretation error,
philosophy, unsupported claim or coach disagreement. No coach study has been executed.
