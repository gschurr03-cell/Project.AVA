# Research copyright and access

AVA stores metadata and citations independently from copyrighted full text. URLs do not
imply permission to download, extract, retain, or redistribute a document.

Allowed future ingestion paths are licensed PDFs, authorized author manuscripts,
verified open-access full text, and internal documents. Every source records access and
license status. Unknown or restricted licenses prevent full-text storage. Abstract access
is never represented as full-paper review.

Research files require a dedicated private bucket, malware scanning, retention rules,
license audit, and reviewer-only signed access before uploads can be enabled. Athlete
reports and public surfaces must never reveal internal object paths or copyrighted text.
