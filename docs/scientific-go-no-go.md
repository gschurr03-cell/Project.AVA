# Scientific go/no-go

Decision date: 2026-07-18. Decision: **NO-GO for athlete-facing scientific beta**.

Internally permitted: FPS/normalization diagnostics, recording guidance, fixture inspection,
and coach review of explicitly limited zone timing, average velocity, combined frequency,
step length and 2D kinematic estimates.

Experimental/hidden: contact time, flight time, projections, high-impact Root Cause,
instantaneous/peak velocity, left-right/asymmetry and precise PB predictions. No injury,
weakness, causation, guaranteed improvement or return-to-play claim is allowed.

Supported capture policy remains single-athlete side-view 60 FPS-class or higher with stable
camera and confirmed zone. No device class is scientifically approved beyond internal
testing. Promotion requires consented locked reference data, pre-registered thresholds,
confidence calibration, blinded coach review and adult comprehension evidence.
