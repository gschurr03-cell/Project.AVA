# Scientific validation study protocol

Version-lock the source video checksum, MediaPipe model, 60 FPS pipeline, metric/rule
registries, calibration, annotation and report versions before evaluation. Pre-register
inclusion/exclusion, reference compatibility and thresholds before opening holdout labels.

Initial targets: at least 30 analyzable trials and 10 athletes for preliminary timing; 300+
dual-reviewed contact events; at least three supported iPhone device classes; paired
60/120/240 captures where possible; high-quality, borderline and rejection blocks; at least
three qualified sprint coaches reviewing 20 cases; at least 10 adult athletes for
comprehension. These are practical preliminary targets, not population generalizability.

Primary references: synchronized timing gates for compatible zone timing; surveyed zones/
tape contact marks for spatial measures; force/pressure or adjudicated high-speed annotation
for contact/flight; marker/reference kinematics where feasible; blinded structured coach
rubrics for interpretation. Consumer wearables are exploratory.

Report bias, MAE, median absolute error, RMSE, limits of agreement and failure rate for
continuous metrics; precision, recall, F1, duplicate rate and frame error for events;
agreement by confidence/FPS/device/quality and bootstrap intervals when sample size permits.
Report missingness and exclusions separately. Correlation never substitutes for agreement.
