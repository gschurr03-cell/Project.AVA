# Internal discovery validation

Biomechanics discoveries enter research as `internal_discovery` sources and preliminary,
unreviewed claims. They are not citations, scientific validation, or athlete advice.

Every proposal requires a holdout dataset, replication, statistical review, confound
analysis, metric-validity review, external evidence comparison, and human expert review.
Supporting and conflicting external claims remain separate. Only an auditable reviewer
decision can change status, and ordinary recommendation/report retrieval excludes the
proposal until production approval.

Internal validation studies—such as AVA versus timing gates, radar, or manual annotation—
use the distinct `internal_validation_study` source type and retain their internal origin.
