# Recording guidance

Use a stable side view with the full measured zone and athlete visible. Avoid zoom, provide
sufficient light, keep the athlete large enough to track, use the highest practical frame rate,
and prefer 60 FPS or higher for authoritative timing. Calibration references must be visible or
configurable; users must not guess a distance.

Browser preflight checks extension, size, duration, resolution, and orientation where metadata
is available. It reports supported, warning, or unsupported. Frame rate and pose quality cannot
be reliably inferred by the browser and remain worker validations. The checklist reduces risk
but never guarantees analysis success.
