# Training Program scientific and medical safety

- No diagnosis, injury determination, medical clearance or rehabilitation protocol.
- Associated muscle groups are not confirmed weakness.
- Acute pain blocks high-intensity work; high pain escalates.
- Clinician restrictions outrank all coaching/preferences.
- Low-confidence or missing safety inputs fail or require review.
- Advanced methods, youth programming and return-to-play are unsupported in v1.
- Every high-intensity item has explicit stopping rules; continuing through pain is forbidden.
- Drafts are unapproved, non-authoritative and cannot activate themselves.

Evidence IDs distinguish expert rules, internal deterministic heuristics and future
research. The fixture does not establish universally optimal dosage. Coach, clinical and
scientific review remain required before athlete exposure.
