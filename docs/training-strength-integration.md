# Strength and power integration

Strength/power work is optional, coordinated with key sprint days, and requires available
facility/equipment and sufficient training age. Dosage supports non-1RM methods when a
validated maximum is absent. Sprint patterns may link to associated qualities but cannot
confirm weakness.

Future AVA Lift data enters through an optional versioned source adapter for history,
maximums, velocity, fatigue, adherence and restrictions. Future Motion IQ findings enter
as optional evidence/restrictions, never automatic diagnosis or medical clearance.
