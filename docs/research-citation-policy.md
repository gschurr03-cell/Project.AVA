# Research citation policy

Citations are generated deterministically from stored metadata. Missing authors,
publication, DOI, PMID, or URL remain missing; the formatter never invents them.

Athlete reports accept only production-approved, athlete-eligible claims and citations.
Coach reports may show greater technical detail but still require production approval.
Internal reasoning may retrieve approved-internal records. Retracted sources never appear
as supporting production citations.

Private full-text object references are never exposed. Page, figure, and table references
belong to evidence links and are included only when reviewers recorded them.
