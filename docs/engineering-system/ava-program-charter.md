# AVA Sprint MVP to Public Launch

Purpose: deliver a scientifically responsible, secure, connected, testable and operational
AVA Sprint product from the audited 56% baseline through closed beta and public launch.

Phases: foundation stabilization; core analysis connection; native vertical slice;
scientific preparation and execution; training connection; coach workflow; closed-beta
readiness and operations; TestFlight/App Store; public launch; postlaunch stabilization.

Scope is AVA Sprint web, worker, native iOS, scientific evidence, deterministic coaching,
training safety and operations. Non-goals are AVA Lift, Motion IQ, recruiting, Android,
subscriptions, autonomous medical/coaching claims and unrelated redesign.

Founder Decision approves product scope, beta claims, cohort, retention and release gates.
Engineering owners implement; security, privacy, science and coaching reviewers may block
release in their domains. No deadline overrides data isolation, athlete safety, scientific
withholding or rollback capability.

Completion uses architecture, implementation, integration, automated testing, real
environment testing, review/validation, operations and release evidence. Code existence is
never sufficient.
