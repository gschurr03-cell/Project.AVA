# Definition of ready

A task is Ready only when outcome, scope/non-goals, parent hierarchy, acceptance criteria,
tests, likely boundaries, owner type, priority/severity, estimate, dependencies, decisions,
environment access, migration/rollout/rollback needs and documentation are known. External
accounts, device, cohort or legal needs must be available or explicitly part of the task.

Otherwise use Blocked, Needs Investigation or Deferred. Ready does not mean approved for
release or safe to skip review.
