# Training interruptions and reentry

The event model captures illness, travel, facility and athlete unavailability, restriction,
and schedule changes. Change scope preserves history and affects only future work. Brief
nonmedical interruptions with good readiness may resume unchanged. Up to seven days
defaults to reduced volume; longer interruptions use an abbreviated reentry microcycle;
poor readiness requires reassessment. Injury or clinician restriction requires clinician
clearance. These are proposed decisions and never apply dosage automatically.
