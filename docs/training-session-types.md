# Training session types and templates

V1 permits acceleration, maximum velocity, tempo, strength/power, recovery, competition
and rest. Advanced resisted, hill, wicket, overspeed, special endurance and rehabilitation
sessions are not enabled merely because they are common.

Templates are stable IDs: `maxv-quality-v1`, `acceleration-quality-v1`,
`acceleration-power-v1`, `tempo-maintenance-v1`, `recovery-v1`, and `competition-v1`.
Ordering places progressive warm-up before sprint work and optional power after sprint
quality. Every high-intensity exercise contains an explicit pain/quality stopping rule.
