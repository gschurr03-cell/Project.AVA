# Longitudinal coaching safety

AVA describes associations, not causation. One good session cannot progress training, one
miss cannot punish or regress it, plateau requires repeated comparable evidence, planned
taper/unload is not regression, and pain blocks progression without diagnosis. Athlete
preference is subordinate to safety and authority. All material revisions remain proposed
until approved.

The longitudinal rollout flag defaults to `DISABLED`. Current evidence is fixture-only:
no coach review, athlete beta, clinician review, longitudinal field validation, or
production proof has occurred.
