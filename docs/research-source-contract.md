# Research source contract

`ResearchSource` preserves bibliographic metadata, stable identifiers, access and license
state, full-text availability, peer-review and study type, ingestion/review states,
corrections, concerns, retractions, supersession, versions, and submission provenance.

Source existence is not claim support. A reviewed `ResearchEvidenceLink` must explain
support type and directness. Abstract-only records are labeled and downgraded. Retracted
records cannot support production retrieval. Private storage references never enter
citations.

Duplicate detection checks DOI, PMID, document hashes, external identifiers, normalized
title, authors, and year. Preprints and final publications remain related versions rather
than silently collapsed records.
