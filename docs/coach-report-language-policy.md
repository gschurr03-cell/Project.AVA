# Coach Report language policy

Reports describe supported evidence, uncertainty, and the next validation step. Athlete
and coach views may differ in detail, but not conclusions.

The report cannot assert diagnosis, proof, certainty, injury risk, danger, dysfunction,
or a guaranteed outcome. It uses confidence and evidence labels from upstream engines.
Unknown context remains unknown. Unavailable evidence is stated explicitly and does not
support priorities.

Medical or rehabilitative direction remains outside the MVP report. Existing stop
conditions and contraindication notes stay in the recommendation contract and are not
expanded by the report composer.
