# Training readiness

Signals include pain, soreness, fatigue, sleep, stress, motivation, illness, workload and
coach restriction, each with source, confidence, timestamp, validity and affected domain.
Outcomes are proceed, reduce volume/intensity, replace, recovery only, coach/medical review
or blocked. Acute pain blocks; high pain escalates; high fatigue/illness permits recovery
only. No symptom becomes a diagnosis and stale data triggers review.
