# Research claim contract

`ResearchClaim` represents one bounded proposition. It stores original and normalized
wording, claim type, population, events, phases, metrics, interventions, direction,
causal boundary, evidence grade and reasons, consensus, applicability, limitations,
excluded conclusions, supporting/conflicting links, review audit, version, and audience
eligibility.

Association claims retain `association_only` causal strength. Candidate and internal
discovery claims begin unreviewed, preliminary or unavailable, and ineligible for coach
or athlete output. Production eligibility requires explicit human review and eligible
non-retracted evidence.
