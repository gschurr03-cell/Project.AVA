# Legal content status

`/privacy` and `/terms` are clearly labeled drafts. `/disclaimer` contains the centralized
scientific/medical boundary. `/data-retention` describes actual current retention and deletion
behavior. `/account` exposes a structured deletion request, and `/support` exposes privacy and
manual-export categories.

No legal page is represented as attorney-reviewed. Versioned Terms/Privacy acceptance is not
yet collected; the existing video-biomechanics consent and onboarding scientific acknowledgement
are not substitutes for legal acceptance. Model-improvement consent is absent and defaults to no
training use. External beta requires counsel/policy approval, versioned legal acceptance, a
retention schedule, deletion SLA, and a monitored support process.
