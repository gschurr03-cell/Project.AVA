# Benchmark comparison policy

Every comparison names its population and dataset version. Sex, event, phase, metric
definition, measurement protocol, technology, timing system, and FPS class are hard
compatibility requirements. A mismatch blocks the comparison.

Age, height, weight, competition level, performance range, training age, surface, and
environment refine population similarity. Unknown optional context lowers matching
confidence; it is never silently assumed.

Percentiles require an active verified production-approved dataset and at least two
reviewed percentile landmarks. Values are linearly interpolated between landmarks and
bounded by the supplied distribution. No extrapolated elite claim is generated.

Strength and development labels are descriptive relative positions only. They do not
diagnose a limitation, prescribe training, or imply that matching an elite fingerprint
is appropriate.
