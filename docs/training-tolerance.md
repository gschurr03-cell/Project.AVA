# Training tolerance

Tolerance is separate from adherence and absence of pain. Acceleration, maximum velocity,
endurance, plyometrics, lifting, density, competitions, and weekly duration are evaluated
independently. Zero comparable exposures remain unknown; fewer than three are low evidence;
repeated comparable completion may be tolerated or well tolerated; a large load change or
one adverse response approaches the limit; repeated adverse response exceeds it; active
restriction temporarily reduces it. Absence of a pain report alone does not count as
positive tolerance evidence. These thresholds require coach validation before they can
authorize progression.
