# Recording robustness results

Engineering fixtures cover camera classification, panning, zoom withholding, tracking loss,
recording-quality gates and unsupported FPS. The operating envelope is conservatively:
single fully visible athlete, side view, landscape, adequate light/contrast, stable static
camera, confirmed zone, and 60 FPS-class or higher. Distance, shake, lighting, occlusion,
compression, device and quality-score calibration studies remain missing.
