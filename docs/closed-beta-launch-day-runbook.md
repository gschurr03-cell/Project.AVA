# Closed-beta launch-day runbook

Only use after every hard gate passes.

Freeze changes; record release/artifact hashes, migrations, flags and rollback versions;
verify backups; run synthetic auth/profile/upload/analysis/report/approved-plan/readiness/
event workflows; provision the smallest allowlisted adult cohort and authorized coaches;
confirm support/incident channels; watch API, queues, workers, storage, scientific integrity,
training safety, native crashes and costs.

Do not expand on day one. Pause growth on SLO breach or repeated workflow failure. Disable
the affected cohort on integrity/safety risk. Suspend beta on authorization/privacy/data-loss
events. End the day with incident, queue, safety, support, cost and gate review.
