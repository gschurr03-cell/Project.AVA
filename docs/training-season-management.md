# Competition-season management

Mesocycle competition records use coach/athlete-confirmed dates and map existing importance
to low, secondary, primary, or championship priorities. Priority affects taper/competition
week selection. The v1 season snapshot validates season dates, typed preparation,
competition and transition blocks, scheduled/completed/cancelled status, travel,
availability, and deterministic fingerprints. Qualification logic, multi-day rounds,
consecutive-meet optimization, time zones, and full indoor/outdoor transitions remain
unvalidated.
