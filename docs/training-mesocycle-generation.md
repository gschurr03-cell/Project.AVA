# Mesocycle generation

The deterministic generator supports two through six weeks. It validates the existing
program input, selects explicit week evolution, generates each child seven-day draft,
inserts review conditions, applies bounded exposure intent, protects competition/taper
context, and fingerprints the complete draft. Child plan IDs are deterministic.

Evolution may be introduction, accumulation, development, consolidation, overload, unload,
taper, competition, recovery, or reassessment. The fixture uses at most an 8% weekly load
increase and three high-intensity days. Every output remains coach-required and draft.
Cross-week physiological validation is fixture-level and requires professional review.
