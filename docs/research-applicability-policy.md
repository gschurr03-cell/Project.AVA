# Research applicability policy

Applicability is evaluated independently from evidence quality. The initial model records
population, event, phase, metric, and intervention matches. It returns directly,
broadly, partially, weakly, not, or unknown applicability with reasons and trace.

A strong study may be inapplicable because its population, phase, metric definition, or
protocol differs. A required `not_applicable` dimension blocks retrieval for that
context. Missing athlete context produces `unknown`, never an assumed match.

Future reviewed dimensions include sex, age, training and competition level, surface,
footwear, environment, resisted status, camera mode, FPS, timing technology, injury
status, and measurement protocol.
