# AVA drill and cue library

## Cues

The versioned cue library contains conservative plain-language cues for:

- preserving torso behavior;
- controlled transition posture;
- relaxed front-side recovery without forced height;
- even bilateral rhythm without forced cadence;
- compatible recording setup.

Restricted cues such as reaching farther, forcing knee height, “punching” the ground, or
running on the toes are not included.

## Drills

The initial library contains:

- controlled wall march;
- low-intensity A-march;
- low-intensity dribble progression;
- low-intensity wicket rhythm with coach-selected spacing;
- bilateral rhythm rehearsal.

All drills are low or submaximal. Each entry defines phase, difficulty, equipment, setup,
execution, cues, common errors, contraindications, stop conditions, progressions,
regressions, supervision needs, evidence basis, and enabled state.

The Recommendation Engine exposes at most one drill-bearing action per duplicate family.
It does not emit every plausible drill.

## Evidence boundary

Current drill evidence is marked limited or heuristic because no verified research
reference layer is integrated. No fake citations or scientific-certainty claims are
displayed.

Advanced or maximal drills from the legacy exercise library are not used by this engine.
