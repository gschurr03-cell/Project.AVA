# Evidence grading policy

AVA grades evidence across study design, biomechanics relevance, directness, sample size,
peer-review status, access to full methods, consistency, corrections, retractions, and
review status. Publication type alone never determines the grade.

Grades are strong, moderate, limited, preliminary, heuristic, conflicting, and
unavailable. Reasons and a calculation trace are mandatory. Abstract-only access,
unreported samples, indirectness, supersession, and expressions of concern lower the
grade. Retraction and missing human review make evidence unavailable.

Consensus is separate. One study can support a claim but cannot establish consensus.
Conflicting reviewed links produce mixed or disputed status and remain visible.
