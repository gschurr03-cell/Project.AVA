# Incident response plan

SEV-1: athlete safety, material privacy/security/data loss or integrity breach—suspend beta
immediately. SEV-2: major workflow outage or repeated incorrect results—contain within 30
minutes. SEV-3: degraded bounded workflow. SEV-4: low-risk defect.

Roles: incident commander, technical lead, security/privacy or science/training lead, and
communications owner. Preserve evidence/audit, revoke credentials/packages when relevant,
contain, restore, verify and document. Safety examples include unapproved/stale execution,
restriction bypass or lost pain event; containment may pause plans, revoke packages, disable
catalog/rule/cohort and notify the authorized coach. Staffing/on-call contacts are not
assigned, so continuous emergency monitoring is not promised.
