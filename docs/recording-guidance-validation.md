# Validation-aligned recording guidance

For the current supported production pipeline: record one athlete in landscape at 60
FPS-class or higher; prefer a stable tripod side view perpendicular to the lane; keep the
whole athlete and confirmed zone visible with approach/exit buffer; avoid zoom/pan, shake,
backlight, occlusion and background runners; use adequate lighting and clothing contrast.

AVA does not claim “record from anywhere.” Higher source FPS is preserved and sampled onto
the validated 60 Hz analysis timeline; it does not unlock unfinished high-speed production
models. A failed quality or ambiguity gate should request a new recording rather than invent
metrics.
