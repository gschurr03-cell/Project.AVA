# Training exercise catalog

The governed v1 catalog contains six approved controlled entries: progressive warm-up,
20 m standing acceleration, fly 20 m, extensive tempo, RPE-based trap-bar jump and mobility/
recovery. Entries include stable ID/version/status, purpose/objectives, associations,
facilities/equipment, training age, cues/errors, restrictions, typed dosage and evidence IDs.

Governance states are draft, review, approved, deprecated and retired. Material dosage,
purpose, progression or safety changes require a catalog version. Historical snapshots
retain their original version. Unknown/unapproved IDs fail validation.
