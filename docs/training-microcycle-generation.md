# Deterministic microcycle generation

V1 produces seven calendar days for three-to-six available training days. Unavailable days
become explicit rest. It allocates primary and secondary key sessions, maintenance/tempo,
recovery and protected competitions while enforcing key-day separation and a maximum of
three high-intensity days.

Planned exposure is separated into acceleration, maximum velocity, tempo and total sprint
distance. Completed and adjusted values enter through adherence/revision events rather than
mutating the original snapshot. High-low organization is configurable rule policy, not
claimed doctrine.
